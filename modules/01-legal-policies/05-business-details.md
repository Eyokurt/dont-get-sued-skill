# Audit Module 05: Business Details & Legal Notice (Impressum)

## 1. Legal Framework & Regulatory Exposure

- **Statutory Frameworks**:
  - **EU eCommerce Directive (Directive 2000/31/EC, Article 5)**: Commercial providers must render easily, directly, and permanently accessible specific corporate identity details to recipients and authorities.
  - **German Digital Services Act (DDG § 5, formerly TMG § 5 - Impressumspflicht)**: Strict legal notice mandate for commercial digital services targeting or accessible to German/EU users.
  - **UK Companies Act 2006 & Consumer Protection Regulations**: Mandatory disclosure of full registered corporate name, registered office address, and company registration number on digital platforms.
  - **FTC Business Transparency Standards**: Prevention of deceptive trade practices through clear corporate identity disclosure.
- **Enforcement & Liability**:
  - Cease-and-desist warnings with immediate legal penalty fees (*Abmahnung* in Germany/Austria, often €1,000–€5,000+ per occurrence); administrative regulatory fines up to €50,000.

---

## 2. Architectural Verification Scope (Neyi Kontrol Etmeli)

- **Corporate Identity Transparency**:
  - Verify that the platform renders full legal corporate identification, including the official legal entity name and structure (e.g. LLC, Inc., GmbH, Ltd., A.Ş.).
- **Physical Registered Address**:
  - Verify that a physical registered office street address is provided (P.O. boxes or virtual mail forwarding without physical presence are legally invalid in jurisdictions with strict Impressum rules).
- **Direct & Rapid Contact Channels**:
  - Verify that direct electronic and telephonic contact channels are disclosed (e.g. verified corporate email address and active customer phone number or real-time electronic contact interface).
- **Registry & Regulatory Identification**:
  - Verify that official company registration details are disclosed (Trade Registry / Chamber of Commerce number, registration court).
  - Verify that Value Added Tax identification numbers (VAT ID / USt-IdNr / TIN) are displayed where issued.

---

## 3. Violation Red Flags (İhlal Kriterleri)

- Commercial web service operating anonymously with zero corporate identity disclosures.
- Relying exclusively on an anonymous contact form without providing a direct, functioning corporate email address.
- Using a generic pseudonym or brand nickname instead of the registered legal entity name.
- Omitting commercial registry numbers, court of registration, or VAT identification numbers on commercial sites targeting European customers.
- Burying corporate details behind multi-step navigation instead of providing a 2-click accessible link in the primary footer or legal navigation.

---

## 4. Compliance Benchmarks (Uyum Standardı)

- Permanent, 2-click accessible navigation link labeled "Legal Notice", "Impressum", or "Company Details" available across all views.
- Unambiguous disclosure of:
  1. Full legal corporate name and entity structure.
  2. Physical registered office address (street, city, postal code, country).
  3. Authorized legal representatives (Directors / Vorstand / Geschäftsführer).
  4. Commercial registry court and registration number.
  5. VAT ID / Tax identification number.
  6. Rapid communication channels: direct telephone number and corporate email address.

---

## 5. Remediation Principles (Çözüm İlkeleri)

- Create a dedicated public route or accessible view for the legal notice and link it prominently in global navigation.
- Ensure corporate entity information is verified against official government registry filings before publication.
- Ensure contact channels provided are actively monitored by the business.
