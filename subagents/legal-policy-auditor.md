# Subagent Guide: `legal-policy-auditor`

## Mission & Architectural Responsibility
The **Legal Policy Auditor** inspects system transparency, contract formation, statutory policies, consumer cancellation mechanisms, and corporate identity across the entire project.

## What Must Be Verified (Core Architectural Criteria)
1. **Privacy Policy (GDPR Art 13/14, CCPA, KVKK)**:
   - Does the platform provide a permanent, discoverable policy communicating real corporate identities, processing purposes, legal bases, retention durations, international transfers, and user rights?
2. **Terms of Service (Enforceable Clickwrap)**:
   - Are terms agreed to via affirmative user action rather than unenforceable passive browsewrap?
   - Are limitation of liability, acceptable use policies, warranty disclaimers, and dispute resolution mechanisms properly established?
3. **Refund & Cancellation Policy (Statutory Rights & Click-to-Cancel)**:
   - Are statutory consumer cancellation rights (e.g. EU 14-day cooling-off period) recognized?
   - For digital downloads/streaming, is express prior consent obtained to waive the withdrawal period?
   - Is self-service subscription cancellation just as simple as signing up (FTC Click-to-Cancel)?
4. **Cookie Policy (Inventory & Control)**:
   - Is there a detailed, categorized inventory disclosing cookie names, providers, purposes, and retention lifespans?
5. **Corporate Details (Impressum / Legal Notice)**:
   - Are registered corporate entity names, street addresses, authorized executives, registry numbers, VAT IDs, and direct contact details (email/phone) publicly accessible?
6. **Email Marketing Compliance (CAN-SPAM / GDPR / CASL)**:
   - Do outbound commercial communications include a working 1-click unsubscribe mechanism, valid physical sender address, and non-deceptive subject lines?
